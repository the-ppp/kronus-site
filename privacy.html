Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using Our Service, We may collect the following personally identifiable information:

  • Email address (if You create an account)
  • Display name (optional, set by You)
  • Authentication provider (Apple, Google, or email/password)
  • Usage Data

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service. Usage Data may include information such as the screens You visit, the time and date of Your visit, the time spent on those screens, unique device identifiers, and other diagnostic data. This may include recordings of Your sessions in the App, with text fields and other sensitive content masked.

Habit Data

The core purpose of the App is to help You build habits. To provide this functionality, We collect and store:

  • The habits You create (name, icon, colour, goal, schedule and reminder settings)
  • Your daily completion records, progress and XP
  • Your preferences and vacation days

If You used an earlier version of the App, Your time-tracking logs are also kept. This data is stored locally on Your Device and, once a session is established, synced to Our cloud database so You can restore it across devices.

Onboarding Data

When You complete the onboarding flow, Your responses to the setup quiz are collected (for example Your goals, habits and what gets in the way of Your consistency). This data is used to personalize Your experience and is stored on Our servers.

Community Data

If You use the Community, the posts and comments You submit are visible to other users of the App, together with Your display name (or "Anonymous" if You have not set one) and Your level badge. Your upvotes count toward a post's public total but are not shown with Your name. We also store reports and blocks You make, and Your time zone (to apply daily posting limits). Reported content is sent to Us for review and may be hidden or removed.

Subscription Data

When You purchase a subscription, We record Your subscription status, product identifier, and relevant timestamps (trial start date, subscription start date, expiry date).

Feedback Data

If You submit feedback through the in-app feedback form, We collect the email address You provide and the content of Your message.

Advertising and Measurement Data

We advertise Kronus on Meta platforms, meaning Facebook and Instagram. So that We can tell which advertisements actually lead people to install and subscribe, the App includes the Meta SDK, which collects:

  • Your Advertising Identifier, but only if You grant permission when asked
  • Application events, namely that the App was installed and opened, that an anonymous session was created, that a free trial was started, and that a subscription was purchased
  • The price and currency of a subscription You purchase
  • Basic technical information about Your Device, such as its model and operating system version

We do not send Your activity logs, Your onboarding responses, Your display name, or Your email address to Meta or to any other advertising partner.

Use of Your Personal Data

The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service, including to monitor the usage of our Service and sync Your data across devices.
  • To manage Your Account: to manage Your registration as a user of the Service. The Personal Data You provide gives You access to cloud backup and cross-device restore functionality.
  • For the performance of a contract: the development, compliance and undertaking of the subscription purchase You make through the Service.
  • To contact You: to send You transactional communications related to Your account or subscription (e.g. email confirmation links, password reset links) when necessary.
  • To manage Your requests: to attend and manage feedback or support requests You submit to Us.
  • To measure our advertising: to understand which advertisements lead people to install and subscribe to the App, and to decide where to spend Our advertising budget. This is described in full under "Advertising, Tracking and Your Choices" below.
  • For other purposes: such as data analysis, identifying usage trends, and evaluating and improving Our Service, features, and user experience.

We may share Your personal information in the following situations:

  • With Service Providers: We may share Your personal information with Service Providers to store data, authenticate users, manage subscriptions, and analyze use of our Service.
  • With advertising partners: where You have allowed Tracking, We share Your Advertising Identifier and the application events described above with Meta Platforms for advertising measurement.
  • For business transfers: We may share or transfer Your personal information in connection with, or during negotiations of, any merger, sale of Company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of Our business to another company.
  • With Your consent: We may disclose Your personal information for any other purpose with Your consent.

Anonymous Sessions

When You first launch the App after completing onboarding, the App automatically creates an anonymous session. A randomly generated unique identifier is assigned to You. No email address, name, or other identifying information is collected at this stage.

If You later create an account, Your anonymous data is migrated to Your account. If You uninstall the App without creating an account, Your anonymous session and any data associated with it cannot be recovered.

Advertising, Tracking and Your Choices

Shortly after You first open the App, iOS asks whether Kronus may track You across apps and websites owned by other companies. This is Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Your answer controls whether We may access Your Advertising Identifier, and You are free to refuse.

If You allow Tracking, Your Advertising Identifier is shared with Meta Platforms together with the application events listed above. Meta may use this information to attribute Your installation to a particular advertisement, to measure how Our advertising performs, and for its own advertising purposes in accordance with its own policies.

If You decline, Your Advertising Identifier is not collected and no cross-app Tracking takes place. We still receive measurement through Apple's SKAdNetwork framework, which reports results in aggregated and delayed form and is designed so that individual users cannot be identified. Declining does not restrict any feature of the App, and Kronus behaves identically either way.

You may change Your decision at any time on Your Device, under Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, Our legal basis for this processing is Your consent, given through the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You may withdraw that consent at any time as described above. Withdrawing it does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before You withdrew it.

Kronus contains no advertisements. We do not sell Your Personal Data, and We do not use advertising data to target advertising at You inside the App.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party service providers including Supabase (database and authentication), PostHog (analytics), Superwall (subscription management), and Meta Platforms (advertising measurement). When You sign in with Apple or Google, those providers also process Your authentication request.

Supabase, PostHog and Superwall process data on Our behalf and are contractually required to protect it. We do not permit them to use Your data for their own advertising or to sell it to third parties.

Meta Platforms is different, and We want to be explicit about that difference. Where You have allowed Tracking, Meta acts as an independent controller of the advertising data it receives, and may use that data for its own purposes, including improving its own advertising systems. Its handling of that data is governed by Meta's own privacy policy, available at facebook.com/privacy/policy. If You decline Tracking, no Advertising Identifier is shared with Meta and this paragraph does not apply to You.

Retention of Your Personal Data

The Company will retain Your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use Your Personal Data to the extent necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our legal agreements and policies.

Usage Data is generally retained for a shorter period of time, except when this data is used to strengthen the security or to improve the functionality of Our Service, or We are legally obligated to retain this data for longer periods.

When You delete Your account, all Personal Data associated with Your account — including Your habits and activity logs, community posts and comments, onboarding responses, settings, profile, and subscription records — is permanently deleted from Our servers immediately. This action is irreversible.

Advertising and measurement data that has already been shared with Meta Platforms is retained by Meta under its own retention practices, which We do not control. Deleting Your Kronus account removes Your data from Our servers, but does not delete data already held by Meta. Requests of that kind have to be made to Meta directly.

Delete Your Personal Data

You have the right to delete or request that We assist in deleting the Personal Data that We have collected about You.

Deleting Your account does not reach data already held by Meta Platforms, as explained under "Retention of Your Personal Data" above. To reach that data, contact Meta directly.

Please note that We may need to retain certain information when we have a legal obligation or lawful basis to do so.
