Frequently asked questions

What is Kronus?

Kronus is a discipline and habit tracker for iPhone. You turn your goals into daily habits, check them off every day, earn XP, climb ranks and keep streaks, and see clear weekly, monthly and yearly stats on whether you are actually doing them.

How is Kronus different from other habit trackers?

Kronus is built around discipline rather than motivation. Every completed habit earns XP, completing your whole day earns a bonus, and you climb through ranks. Vacation mode lets you take a planned break without breaking your streak, and the Kronus community lets you share wins and setbacks.

Who is Kronus for?

People who want discipline, not motivation: builders, athletes, students, entrepreneurs, and anyone tired of starting over who wants a clear system for daily execution. Common goals include the gym, money and career, quitting bad habits and building confidence.

Is Kronus free?

Kronus is free to download on the App Store. Kronus Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription. Prices in your currency, and any free trial terms, are shown in the app before you pay. You can cancel any time in your Apple account settings.

Is Kronus available on Android?

Kronus is currently available for iPhone on the App Store.

How do I cancel my Kronus subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Kronus and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid a renewal.
