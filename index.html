STRAIGHT
ANSWERS.

What is Kronus?

A discipline and habit tracker for iPhone. Turn your goals into daily habits, check them off, earn XP, climb ranks and see clear weekly, monthly and yearly stats.

How is it different from other habit trackers?

It's built around discipline, not motivation: XP for every habit, a bonus for a complete day, 28 ranks, streaks, vacation mode and a community.

Is Kronus free?

Free to download. Kronus Premium is an optional subscription; prices and any trial are shown in the app before you pay.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your current streak starts again, but your best streak stays on record and every XP and rank you earned stays yours. Days with nothing scheduled never break a streak.

Can I take a break without losing my streak?

Yes. Vacation mode pauses your habits for the dates you choose, so a trip or a sick week doesn't cost you the streak.

Is my data private?

Your habits sync to your account so you can restore them on a new phone. We don't sell your data, and you can delete your account and everything in it from Settings at any time. More in our Privacy Policy.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Kronus › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid a renewal.

Is Kronus on Android?

Kronus is currently only available for iPhone on the App Store.
