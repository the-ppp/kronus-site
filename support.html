How does Kronus work?

Pick habits from the ready-made library or create your own, and schedule them on the days that matter. Check each one off as you do it. Every completed habit earns XP, a fully completed day earns a bonus, and you climb through ranks while keeping your streaks alive. Your stats show your consistency for every week, month and year.

Is my data stored securely?

Yes. Your habits are stored on your device and synced to our secure cloud database, so you can restore them across devices once you create an account. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I take a break without losing my streak?

Use Vacation mode: in the app go to Settings → Data → Vacations and add the dates of your break. Your streak is kept while you're away.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Kronus and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid a renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Is Kronus available for Android?

Kronus is currently only available for iPhone on the App Store.

How do I delete my data or my account?

To reset everything, go to Settings → Data → Delete All Data. To delete your account and all data linked to it from our servers, go to Settings → Delete Account. Both actions are irreversible.
